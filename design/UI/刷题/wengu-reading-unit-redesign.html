重设计态 · 完整单元

演示态
1/4
A group of Labour MPs, among them Yvette Cooper, are bringing in the new year with a call to institute a UK “town of culture” award. The proposal is that it should sit alongside the existing city of culture title, which was held by Hull in 2017, and has been awarded to Coventry for 2021. Cooper and her colleagues argue that the success of the crown for Hull, where it brought in £220m of investment and an avalanche of arts, ought not to be confined to cities. Britain’s towns, it is true, are not prevented from applying, but they generally lack the resources to put together a bid to beat their bigger competitors. A town of culture award could, it is argued, become an annual event, attracting funding and creating jobs.
Some might see the proposal as a booby prize for the fact that Britain is no longer able to apply for the much more prestigious title of European capital of culture, a sought-after award bagged by Glasgow in 1990 and Liverpool in 2008. A cynic might speculate that the UK is on the verge of disappearing into an endless fever of self-celebration in its desperation to reinvent itself for the post-Brexit world: after town of culture, who knows what will follow—village of culture? Suburb of culture? Hamlet of culture?
It is also wise to recall that such titles are not a cure-all. A badly run “year of culture” washes in and out of a place like the tide, bringing prominence for a spell but leaving no lasting benefits to the community. The really successful holders of such titles are those that do a great deal more than fill hotel bedrooms and bring in high-profile arts events and good press for a year. They transform the aspirations of the people who live there; they nudge the self-image of the city into a bolder and more optimistic light. It is hard to get right, and requires a remarkable degree of vision, as well as cooperation between city authorities, the private sector, community groups and cultural organisations. But it can be done: Glasgow’s year as European capital of culture can certainly be seen as one of a complex series of factors that have turned the city into the powerhouse of art, music and theatre that it remains today.
A “town of culture” could be not just about the arts but about honouring a town’s peculiarities—helping sustain its high street, supporting local facilities and above all celebrating its people. Jeremy Wright, the culture secretary, should welcome this positive, hope-filled proposal, and turn it into action.
6 单选 推理判断
According to Paragraph 2, the proposal might be regarded by some as（ ）
A
an inaccessible target
B
a sensible compromise
C
an eye-catching bonus
D
a self-deceiving attempt
D
第二段中 “booby prize”（安慰奖）暗示该提案被视为一种自欺式的尝试：英国已无法申请“欧洲文化之都”，只好自造名目自我庆祝。A “inaccessible target” 与文意相反；B “sensible compromise” 与 “cynic might speculate” 的讥讽语气不符；C “eye-catching bonus” 无对应。故答案为 D。
7 单选 细节定位
Cooper and her colleagues argue that a “town of culture” award could（ ）
A
restrict entries to towns with enough resources
B
become a yearly event that draws funding and jobs
C
take the place of the city of culture title
D
be judged by a panel of Labour MPs
B
第一段末句直接给出答案：“could, it is argued, become an annual event, attracting funding and creating jobs”。A 与原文相反（原文说小镇“generally lack the resources”，是现状而非限制条件）；C 原文说该奖项与 city of culture 并列存在，不是取代；D 无中生有，文中未出现评审主体。故答案为 B。
8 单选 词义猜测
The phrase “booby prize” (Para. 2) suggests that the proposal is seen as（ ）
A
a consolation for a bigger loss
B
a reward for cultural excellence
C
a threat to European cooperation
D
a waste of public money
A
“booby prize” 指末等奖、安慰奖；同位语从句随即说明理由——英国已无法申请规格更高的“欧洲文化之都”（Glasgow 1990、Liverpool 2008）。因此该提案被看作退而求其次的安慰。B、C、D 在第二段均无对应信息，属无中生有。故答案为 A。
9 单选 主旨推理
What does the author consider crucial to a successful “year of culture”?（ ）
A
Bringing in high-profile arts events
B
Filling hotel rooms and earning good press
C
Changing how local people see themselves
D
Securing investment from the private sector
C
第三段把 “really successful holders” 与只填满酒店、带来曝光的那类作对比，落点是 “They transform the aspirations of the people who live there; they nudge the self-image of the city into a bolder and more optimistic light”，第四段又以上 “celebrating its people” 收束。A、B 恰是被作者降格为“不止于此”的表层指标；D 只是成功所需的协作条件之一，不是关键。故答案为 C。

设计说明 · 取值为底线而非上限

只改材料阅读节奏与题卡内部间距；侧栏、统计条、开刷面板、移动端一律未动。

为什么材料正文用 Georgia 衬线

四段 300~500 词的英文长文，扫读时最需要的是“字行分明”。Georgia 的 x-height 更大、字面更开，在 16px/1.75 下比无衬线更像正文页；同时与题卡的 PingFang SC 无衬线形成层级区分——眼睛一看就知道“这块是要读的，下面那块是要答的”。仅材料正文使用，题干与选项保持无衬线。

行长与两侧留白

正文 max-width:68ch 居中，约 66 字符/行，落在 45~75 字符的舒适区间。主区 1100px 下两侧各约 250px 留白不是浪费：左留白放 ¶ 段落序号（题目会引用 “Paragraph 2”），右留白放 before → after 小字标注。窗口继续变宽时正文不再拉长，留白随之变宽。

分界：从“紧贴虚线”到“两区各自成组”

材料区改为 --surface-inset（surface 降 0.10L）的凹槽，题卡区回到卡面并加大上缘内边距 24px；两者之间只留 1px 渐隐细线（两端透明、中间 26% 暖沙），末段到线 41px。原来的 1px dashed 改为渐隐线后，视线不再被一条等宽的硬边切断。

题卡 8/12/16/20 阶梯

卡头→题干 16 ｜ 题干→选项 16 ｜ 选项行距 8 ｜ 选项→chips 20 ｜ chips→思路 12 ｜ 思路→提交行 20 ｜ 提交行→解析 20，全部走 --sp2/3/4/5 令牌。选项行 min-height:44px（底线 40px，同时满足触控），字母徽标与选项正文用 align-items:baseline 对齐。

交互态：悬停只压深，不降对比

深色暖调里，把悬停做成“压深一档”（背景变暗而非变亮），正文对比随之上升而非下降：选项 5.6:1 → 7.5:1，主按钮暖白字 6.0:1 → 7.7:1。禁用态（如上一步）才允许降低对比。

两处必要的取值修正

① 主按钮文字：令牌 --b3-theme-on-primary（近白）压在 #c5866a 上实测仅 2.90:1，不达 4.5:1；故按钮填充改走同色相压深一档的 --primary-strong #8c5237，暖白字 6.00:1。② 陶土橙作小字压在卡面上仅 3.17:1，故卡面上的橙色文字统一用 --primary-text #e1a990（4.66:1）。两处都不新增色相，只沿 oklch(from …) 调整明度。
